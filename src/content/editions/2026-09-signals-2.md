The back half of September brings one protocol-level shift builders should
plan around, plus a look at where Midnight shows up next on the conference
calendar.

## 1. Mainnet smart contract deployment moves from whitelist to open access

📅 **Announced:** September 20, 2026

Midnight CTO Sebastien Guillemot
[confirmed on X](https://x.com/SebastienGllmt/status/2101183752991088907) that
private smart contract deployment on Mainnet is coming off its current
whitelist.

Today, only a small set of vetted applications (AscendPerps among them) can
run private smart contracts on Mainnet — a "gated garden," in Guillemot's
words. That's changing:

- **Open deployment** — any developer will be able to deploy and experiment
  with private smart contracts on Mainnet, not just whitelisted apps.
- **Private custom tokens** — the upgrade also enables minting private,
  Zcash-style custom tokens, usable in this initial phase in select DeFi
  scenarios through Offer Files.
- **Composability, in a later release** — Guillemot flagged that a
  *subsequent* version of smart contracts will add events and composability,
  letting contracts talk to each other and enabling more complex DeFi (his
  reference point: something like an Aave-style lending protocol).

No firm date was given beyond "soon." Builders currently designing around the
whitelisted model should watch official channels for the exact activation
window rather than assuming open deployment is already live.

## 2. Midnight heads to TOKEN2049 Singapore

📅 **Window:** October 7-8, 2026 — Marina Bay Sands

TOKEN2049 Singapore is coming up, and Midnight has a confirmed anchor point
plus a couple of ongoing projects likely to get airtime around the event.

**Confirmed:**

- **Night Sky Accelerator pitch day** — the 10-week accelerator that kicked
  off in July 2026 [culminates at TOKEN2049](https://midnight.network/blog/night-sky-accelerator),
  where 4-6 selected early-stage teams pitch to a curated audience of VCs and
  ecosystem partners.
- **Charles Hoskinson** is confirmed to speak at TOKEN2049 (listed as
  Founder/CEO of Input Output Group).

**Worth watching, not yet confirmed as TOKEN2049 announcements:**

- **Passport** — Midnight's user-facing identity/wallet layer, aiming for a
  "scan a QR code, land on an authenticated account" flow with no seed phrase
  or gas-token purchase. Development is active, with releases shipping to the
  [`passport-demo`](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/passport-demo) repo
  through September.
- **Midnight City** — the interactive simulation demonstrating selective
  disclosure at scale, live since February 2026 at
  [midnight.city](https://midnight.city/), remains the network's flagship
  public-facing showcase and a likely talking point at any Midnight booth or
  session.

Treat the Passport and Midnight City items as ecosystem context rather than
TOKEN2049-specific news until an official announcement ties them to the
conference — worth checking Midnight's channels again closer to October 7 for
the final agenda.

## The signal behind the updates

Taken together, these two threads point at the same transition: Midnight
moving from a carefully curated, permissioned rollout toward broader, more
public participation — open smart contract deployment for developers on one
side, and a bigger public/investor-facing presence at TOKEN2049 on the other.
