The new Compact toolchain generation targets more sophisticated DApps:

- **Cross-contract calls** — contracts can invoke other contracts.
- **Contract events** — a standardized way for apps and external systems to
  observe contract activity.
- **New serialization / deserialization** capabilities.
- **ZKIR v3** — evolution of the intermediate representation used by the ZK
  proving stack.
- **Ledger 9 compatibility.**

Compatibility caveat: Ledger 9-compatible tooling being available does **not**
mean Ledger 9 is active on Mainnet. Developers targeting the current Mainnet
should keep using the Ledger 8-compatible **Compact 0.31.x** toolchain until the
network upgrade is activated.
