Node 2.1.0 is not a routine maintenance release. It introduces the machinery for
a **live-chain upgrade** from the currently active 1.0.3 chains. At the protocol
level it moves `spec_version` `1_000_003` → `2_001_000` and `transaction_version`
`3` → `4`, and activation performs a **Ledger 8 → Ledger 9 hard fork** with an
on-chain, multi-block state migration.

Ledger 9 is the foundation for the capabilities landing in the new Compact stack
(cross-contract calls, contract events, ZKIR v3). Tooling availability does not
mean Ledger 9 is active on Mainnet yet — watch the Compact / Midnight.js /
Ledger / Node version matrix when you pick your target.
