TOKEN2049 Singapore runs October 7-8, 2026 at Marina Bay Sands. What's
confirmed so far about Midnight's presence:

- **Night Sky Accelerator pitch day** — the 10-week accelerator (started July
  2026) culminates in an in-person pitch event at TOKEN2049, where 4-6
  selected early-stage teams present to a curated audience of VCs and
  ecosystem partners.
- **Charles Hoskinson** is confirmed to speak at TOKEN2049 (listed as
  Founder/CEO of Input Output Group).

Two ongoing Midnight R&D projects are worth watching as likely talking
points around the event, though neither has a confirmed TOKEN2049-specific
announcement yet:

- **Passport** — the user-facing identity/wallet layer (scan a QR code, land
  on an authenticated account with no seed phrase or gas-token purchase).
  Development is active, with releases shipping to the
  [`passport-demo`](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/passport-demo)
  repository through September.
- **Midnight City** — the interactive simulation demonstrating selective
  disclosure at scale, live since February 2026 at
  [midnight.city](https://midnight.city/), remains the network's flagship
  public-facing showcase.

Worth checking Midnight's official channels closer to Oct 7 for the final
agenda and any dedicated announcements tied to the conference.
